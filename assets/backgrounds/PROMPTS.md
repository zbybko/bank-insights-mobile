# Background variants

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Original portrait backgrounds for Bank Insights. The app background has not been switched automatically.

## Shared prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: a finished portrait wallpaper image for the Bank Insights mobile app, roughly 9:19.5 tall smartphone aspect ratio. Generate ONE full-bleed background, never a grid or a phone mockup. Art direction: original premium fintech wallpaper with the restrained, fluid, polished feel associated with Revolut, without copying a specific existing artwork. Deep tonal depth, subtly luminous gradients and very soft sculptural light, sophisticated and calm. Will sit behind white text, translucent blue-gray cards and mint accents: keep the middle and lower areas dark and low contrast with ample visual breathing room, distribute the color mostly in broad edge glows. Beautiful smooth gradients, no harsh edges, no grain, no banding. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, icons, logos, UI components, cards, frames, phones, people, planets, stars or watermark. Opaque background.
